About this project

Raju Apartment by Swarratna Developer — exclusive 3 BHK luxury apartments of 218 sq. yd. (1,962 sq. ft.) on a two-road corner near Ankur Cross Road. A purely residential 7-storey tower with two lifts, two-sided open views that fill the interiors with natural light, and one allotted car parking per apartment.

Photographs

Amenities & Features

  • 12 metre (40 ft.) wide road — prime, well-connected location
  • Purely residential project, no commercial use
  • Two-sided, lifetime open view from the corner for natural light
  • 1 tower, 7-storey building with 2 lifts in the block
  • 3 BHK apartments of 218 sq. yd. (1,962 sq. ft.), 4 units per floor
  • 1 allotted car parking per apartment
  • Indoor games & library
  • Open-air theatre on the terrace
  • Multipurpose hall
  • Gazebo & senior citizen sit-out
